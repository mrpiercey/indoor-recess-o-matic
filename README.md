# Indoor Recess-O-Matic

Live site: https://mrpiercey.github.io/indoor-recess-o-matic/

A giant 16-bit arcade wheel for picking an indoor recess activity. You stand face-on to the wheel: pull the lever on the right (drag it down, click it, or press **Space**) and 20 screen-free games roll past you, beeping as each one goes by and dinging when one lands in the window.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies. Works on a projector or smartboard; use the Fullscreen button.

**Helper screens.** Thirteen of the games open a simple pop-up when you tap "Let's Play!":

- **Word slots** (Speed Doodle, Telephone, Would You Rather, Charades, Pictionary, Stretch & Yoga, 20 Questions): the words roll like a slot machine with the same quiet beeps and stop one at a time with a ding. Every slot has 30 options written to read as one sentence. Secret games (Charades, Pictionary, Telephone, 20 Questions) show the answer with a HIDE / SHOW button so the teacher can cover it once the right student has seen it.
- **Timers**: Hot Potato and Musical Chairs play a beat and buzz at a random moment. Four Corners counts down from 10 and then reveals a corner. Human Knot has a stopwatch that tracks the best time. Mystery Trash has a two-minute cleanup clock, Book Tasting has a three-minute round timer that chimes ROTATE, and the drawing and acting games have 30- or 60-second timers.

Press **Space** inside any pop-up to roll or start.

**Teacher settings** (bottom-left): turn individual activities on or off, mute sound. Choices are saved in the browser.

## Style

Built on the Edutopia palette (Electric Orange, Sunshine, Pacific Blue, Midnight Blue, and friends) and the edu bug, rendered Sega Genesis style: the wheel is drawn at one-third resolution and upscaled with pixelated scaling, with pixel fonts (Press Start 2P, VT323), hard-stop shading, dithering, and scanlines.
